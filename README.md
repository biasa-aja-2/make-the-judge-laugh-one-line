## The line

> I asked the barista for something strong, so she handed me her resignation letter.

## Why this line

The setup primes one meaning of "something strong" — a strong coffee — and the punchline cashes in a completely different one: the boldest thing the barista has, her resignation letter. It is a joke a stranger gets in one read, every word earns its place, the laugh lands on the last word, and it is not a stock pun anyone has heard before.

## How it works

`main.py` (pure Python stdlib, no dependencies) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`): a single line, 82 characters, valid UTF-8, no trailing newline.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
